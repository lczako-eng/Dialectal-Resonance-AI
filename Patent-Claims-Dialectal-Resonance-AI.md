# Patent Claims – Dialectal Resonance AI Framework
# Author: Laszlo Czako
# Year: 2025
# Status: Patent Pending – All Claims Reserved

This document defines the patent-pending computational and algorithmic claims for the
Dialectal Resonance Principle (DRP) as applied to artificial intelligence systems.

## Claim 1 — Dialectal Imprint Modeling Engine
A system that identifies, extracts, and constructs an internal representation of a user's
“developmental dialect,” based on linguistic patterns learned approximately between ages
12–25.

## Claim 2 — Dual-Receiver Inference Architecture
A bi-channel inference model that separates input processing into:
(a) a semantic-logical receiver, and  
(b) a dialect-identity emotional receiver,
and weights outputs based on dialectal alignment scores.

## Claim 3 — Dialectal Resonance Scoring Algorithm
A computational method that assigns a “Dialectal Resonance Score” to any message by
matching the message’s linguistic features against the user’s stored developmental dialect.

## Claim 4 — Adaptive Dialect Transformation Module
An AI system that transforms or rewrites a message into the user's developmental dialect—
without changing semantic intent—using a mapping engine and dialect-specific tone,
rhythm, slang, pacing, and idiomatic markers.

## Claim 5 — Emotional Absorption Prediction Model
A predictive model that estimates the probability of emotional acceptance, internalization,
or compliance based on the Dialectal Resonance Score.

## Claim 6 — Multi-Audience Dialectal Broadcasting System
A system that rewrites a single message into multiple dialectal variants simultaneously for
different demographic groups, age cohorts, or identity clusters.

## Claim 7 — AI Negotiation & De-Escalation Engine Using DRP
An AI negotiation engine that detects mismatched dialect-emotional inputs and adjusts its
linguistic strategy to increase cooperation or reduce conflict based on DRP scoring.

## Claim 8 — DRP-Based Adaptive Tutoring System
An instructional AI that rewrites lessons into each learner’s dialectal imprint to enhance
retention, comprehension, and emotional engagement.
